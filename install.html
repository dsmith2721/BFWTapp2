Feelwell(beta)

Find your word. Then make a move.

Feelwell is a free feelings wheel. Find the word for what you feel, then pick a healthy next step. It works right in your browser, and you can put it on your mobile device's home screen like any other app.

Open Feelwell

Put Feelwell on your home screen

There's nothing to download from an app store. Pick your device and follow the steps.

  1. Open in Safari

    Open this page in Safari, then tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen: a square with an arrow pointing up.

    Don't see it? Tap the ••• button first, then Share.

  2. Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.

  3. Tap Add

    Leave the name as Feelwell and tap Add in the top-right corner.

  4. You're all set

    Feelwell is on your home screen. Tap the wheel to open it, just like an app.

  1. Open in Chrome

    Open this page in Chrome, then tap the three dots in the top-right corner.

  2. Install the app

    Tap Add to home screen. On some phones it says Install app.

  3. Tap Install

    Tap Install (or Add) to confirm.

  4. You're all set

    Feelwell is on your home screen. Tap the wheel to open it.

  1. Click the install icon

    In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar: a small screen with a down arrow.

    On a Mac with Safari, choose File, then Add to Dock.

  2. Click Install

    Click Install. Feelwell opens in its own window and gets a shortcut on your computer.

What's inside

  • A four-ring feelings wheel: from a core feeling to a healthy next step
  • Plain-language meanings next to every word
  • A tally and weekly view that show your patterns over time
  • Gentle reminders, and backups you control
  • English and Spanish, with larger text and a words-only mode
